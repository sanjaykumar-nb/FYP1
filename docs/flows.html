Three flows through TeamSync

What a manager does, what one analysis actually does across the two services, and what happens when work lands in GitHub. Every number in the product is computed from a knowledge graph of the project, and every warning carries the tasks and people it came from — these three pictures are where that shows up.

The rule underneath all three

Nothing in this chain asks a language model whether a risk exists. Risks are computed from the graph, each finding carries the node ids that witness it, and those ids are checked against the graph before anyone sees them — 40,001 of 40,001 resolved across 2,961 analyses of real sprints. That is also why a status change driven by GitHub keeps the commit that caused it.
